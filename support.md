# Hivestorm Support

Hivestorm is a neon arcade shooter for iPhone, for one player or two players online, made by one person: Stian Andre Olsen.

Questions, bugs, ideas, or an offensive nickname on the high scores? Email **st-aolse@online.no**. Please tell me which iPhone and which version of Hivestorm you have (it's shown on the App Store page and in the iPhone's Settings › General › iPhone Storage › Hivestorm).

## How to play

- **Steer:** drag anywhere in the lower part of the screen. Your ship moves with your finger, so your thumb doesn't hide it.
- **Shoot:** your ship fires on its own.
- **Smart bomb:** double-tap anywhere.
- **Shop:** after every second stage and after each boss, spend the coins you collected on upgrades.
- **Pause:** the pause button is at the top right in solo games. Co-op games can't be paused, because your partner is still playing.

## Playing co-op

Both phones need an internet connection and the same version of Hivestorm. If one of you can't connect, update the game from the App Store on both phones first.

1. One player taps **HOST CO-OP**. A join code and a QR code appear.
2. The other player scans the QR code with the iPhone's Camera app, or taps **JOIN CO-OP** and types the code.
3. The game starts as soon as the second player is in.

Anyone who has the code can join until the game starts, so only share it with the person you want to play with.

**Can't connect?** Check that both phones are online, then let the host start a new game to get a fresh code. Some public or school Wi-Fi networks block online games; try mobile data instead.

## Online high scores

Posting scores online is optional. Nothing is posted until you choose a nickname under **HIGH SCORES** and tap **SAVE & GO ONLINE**.

- **Change your nickname:** HIGH SCORES → CHANGE.
- **Delete everything online:** HIGH SCORES → CHANGE → **DELETE MY ONLINE DATA**, then tap it again to confirm.
- **Report a nickname:** offensive names are refused automatically, but if one gets through, email me the nickname and which list it's on, and I'll remove it.

## Privacy

Hivestorm has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking, and the game adds no analytics of its own. The [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) explains what Unity's online services handle.
